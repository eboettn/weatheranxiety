# Branding, Marketing, Writing Style, and ICP

## Brand Personality
The brand should feel:
- Trustworthy
- Calm
- Warm
- Modern
- Intelligent
- Confidence-building
- Family-friendly

## What The Brand Should Avoid
- Fear-based weather media
- Doom-and-gloom language
- Overly technical meteorology
- Childish presentation
- Therapy-app energy

## Primary ICPs

### Parents of Weather-Anxious Children
Pain points:
- Child fear during storms
- Not knowing how to explain weather
- Anxiety escalation

Desired outcomes:
- Confidence
- Understanding
- Better communication

### Weather-Anxious Adults
Pain points:
- Fear of storms
- Radar anxiety
- Catastrophic interpretation

Desired outcomes:
- Clarity
- Confidence
- Timing understanding

### General Families
Pain points:
- Confusing weather apps
- Information overload

Desired outcomes:
- Faster understanding
- Practical weather guidance

## Writing Style
Tone should be:
- Calm
- Clear
- Conversational
- Scientifically grounded
- Emotionally intelligent

Avoid:
- Hype
- Clickbait
- Catastrophic phrasing
- Jargon overload

## SEO Strategy
Focus areas:
- weather anxiety
- helping kids with storms
- understanding severe weather
- understanding radar
- what alerts mean
- storm timing

## Content Marketing
Potential topics:
- What a tornado watch really means
- Understanding SPC outlooks
- Helping kids during storms
- Why forecasts change
