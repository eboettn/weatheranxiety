# Founder Story and E-E-A-T

## Founder Background
Eric Matthew Boettner is an executive operations leader, nonprofit executive, strategist, husband, father, and weather enthusiast.

He currently serves as Vice President of Operations and Advancement for Lily Pad - A Soft Place to Land.

## Why This Platform Exists
The platform was inspired by helping Eric’s son navigate weather anxiety.

Dark clouds, radar imagery, storms, and weather alerts often created fear and uncertainty.

Through helping his son better understand:
- radar
- storm timing
- alerts
- watches vs warnings

Eric saw anxiety decrease significantly.

## Core Belief
Understanding reduces fear.

## E-E-A-T Strategy

### Experience
- Real parenting experience

### Expertise
